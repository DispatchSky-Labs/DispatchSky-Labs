The program

Useful experience. Honest perspective.

Who it is for

People interested in ADX preparation and better study tools. You do not need to be a current app user to apply.

Thoughtful written feedback and occasional study check-ins. Some participants may be offered a conversation with the aircraft dispatcher who created the app.

What selected members receive

Complimentary access to the full Flight Dispatch Study question bank during participation, with possible early access to future work.

Flight Dispatch Study is already publicly available. “Founding Circle” refers to the first research cohort, not a pre-release beta.
